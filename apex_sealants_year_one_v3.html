Apex Sealants: Year One

A small-group management simulation. Take over a struggling industrial sealants division, set priorities, respond to events, and prepare for your year-end review.

Opening Briefing

You have recently been assigned to manage Apex Sealants, a small division inside Northstar Industrial Materials. Apex produces industrial sealants used in manufacturing, infrastructure repair, equipment maintenance, and specialized construction applications.

The division has underperformed for several years. Senior leadership believes the product line may still have value, but they are concerned about declining customer retention, shrinking market share, inconsistent execution, and weak coordination across the team.

You have been given one year to stabilize the division and present your results to your boss. Each quarter, you will set priorities, respond to events, and receive a briefing on the state of the business.

Market Conditions

The industrial sealants market is becoming more competitive. Customers are asking for better performance under heat, pressure, vibration, chemical exposure, and rapid installation conditions. Procurement teams are also pushing hard on cost. Apex has historically competed on reliability, technical support, and long-term relationships, but several competitors are now promising stronger products at lower prices.
