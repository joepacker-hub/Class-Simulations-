Opening Briefing

You are the mayor of North Valley, a small town whose identity is closely tied to pomegranate farming. The annual Pomegranate Festival brings visitors downtown, supports local vendors, gives farmers a public showcase, and provides a visible measure of confidence in town leadership.

This year's festival matters. Businesses want stronger foot traffic, residents want a family-friendly event, council members want financial discipline, and farmers want assurance that the town still values the crop that made the festival possible.

You have four planning rounds before the festival outcome is clear. Each round, your group will rank mayoral priorities, review updates, and respond to emerging problems.
